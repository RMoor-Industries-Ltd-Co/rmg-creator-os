# Master Atelier — HVN Havenry Room Production Standard

> Production-side implementation standard for the HVN Havenry showroom. The canonical
> consumer showroom visual authority remains
> `hvnhavenry-com/docs/ROOM_VISUAL_DOCTRINE.md`.

## Purpose

Master Atelier must generate Havenry showroom rooms as governed layered assets, not as
single flattened ecommerce images. Production is **room-first**: establish a credible
private environment, then add the Appointment and independent Impression layers.

## Required production package

Each room candidate must deliver:
1. clean product-free room background
2. isolated Appointment PNG with transparency
3. isolated PNG for every Impression
4. transparent combined foreground product layer
5. review composite
6. manifest containing source, placement, camera, and status metadata

## Visual production constraints

The room must feel inhabited in spirit but unoccupied in composition. Prefer cultivated
private-environment language: dark/natural woods, aged metals, leather/textile, plaster,
stone, believable practical/window/fire light, natural contrast, and restraint.

Do not default to glossy black-marble/gold hotel-lobby imagery, symmetrical LED luxury,
sterile showroom lighting, generic penthouse staging, or impossible architecture.

## Camera package

Every candidate records:
- camera position in room
- camera height
- camera angle
- approximate full-frame-equivalent lens
- horizon/vertical treatment
- intended depth hierarchy

Production should vary threshold, corner, long-axis, seated, table-height, low
architectural, foreground-framed, inward/outward terrace, and restrained elevated views.
Do not repeatedly use the same eye-level three-quarter tripod composition.

Use approximately 28–35 mm equivalent for most room establishing views, 35–50 mm for
intimate room views, and 50–85 mm for compressed placement/object studies unless a
different lens is deliberately justified.

## Physical-believability QA

Reject candidates with warped geometry, scale mismatch, impossible circulation,
nonfunctional cabinetry/doors/fireplaces, contradictory light/shadows/reflections,
floating objects, fused furniture, or strong ultra-wide distortion.

A render should feel photographed before it feels generated.

## Staging QA

Before adding products, mark:
- Appointment zone
- two required Impression zones where room/function permits
- one optional future Impression zone
- hover-silhouette clearance
- safe negative space for UI and responsive cropping

Prime commerce surfaces must not be permanently cluttered by non-clickable décor.

## Product integration

Appointments and Impressions remain independent alpha assets. A practical pairing is
mandatory: products must occupy locations where a real owner would use them.

The Appointment normally carries visual hierarchy. Impressions support it without
turning the room into a catalog grid.

## Review handoff

No candidate is promoted from staging to approved until it passes:
- Havenry Room Visual Doctrine
- source/product fidelity
- camera realism
- architectural realism
- layer completeness
- hotspot/hover readiness
- composition distinctiveness
- founder visual approval where required by the governing production contract

## Authority boundary

This file translates the Havenry room doctrine into Master Atelier production behavior.
It does not redefine Havenry vocabulary, consumer interaction contracts, HVN Global
editorial doctrine, or AMGPx project precedence.
